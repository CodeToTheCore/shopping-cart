# Brand Partners Tracker

I work with the **brand companies directly**, not stores. Update this as I reach out.

## Three ways to partner with a brand

| Deal | How it works | What I earn | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Affiliate** | I link to the brand's site; they handle the sale | A percentage of each sale (often around 3–10% for apparel) | None: no money up front |
| **Creator / ambassador** | They give my audience a discount code and track sales by code | A percentage, sometimes free product | None |
| **Wholesale** | I buy their products at a wholesale price and resell them myself | The difference between wholesale and retail | I pay up front and hold inventory |

Wholesale usually needs a **business registration** (an LLC or a DBA), a **resale certificate** from New York State so I don't pay sales tax on stock I resell, and a **minimum first order**. Ask each brand what their minimums are.
